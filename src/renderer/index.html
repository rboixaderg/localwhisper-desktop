LocalWhisper

Local speech-to-text with custom whisper.cpp models

Configuration

Sent with --prompt, capped at 600 characters by the app. Keep the list short: whisper keeps the last ~224 tokens of the submitted prompt. The token bound is conservative, not an exact count.

    Applied to the final text, whole words only and case-insensitive. Works with any model.

    Click the field and press a key combination. At least one modifier is required.

    Off by default. When on, each completed dictation is kept on this device: the audio (WAV), the raw Whisper transcript and the final text. Audio recordings can contain private information. Nothing is uploaded or stored in config.json.
